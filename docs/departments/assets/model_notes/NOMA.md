# Noma — Current 3D Model Notes

Use the **current Cato 3D model** for shared mechanical/material language, not the old Noma PNG body.

## Identity target
- compact, softer field-science/research chassis
- cream/off-white shell with forest/olive green identity accents
- dark graphite internals
- cyan/teal visor display
- **leaf-shaped antenna/fins are a key permanent identity cue**
- slightly more rounded/organic-inspired panel shapes than the other citizens while remaining clearly mechanical

## Proportion target
- compact and short, approximately 1.60 m presentation cue
- lighter/softer silhouette than Bex despite similar overall height
- avoid Cato-like shoulder bulk
- avoid Iri-like razor-thin elegance

## Personality read
Gentle curiosity, patient observation, the citizen who notices small details and wants to understand why they matter.

## Leaf-antenna rule
The leaf shapes are design language, not literal biology, plant growth, sensing capability, or environmental authority.

## Do not bake in
- plants/samples
- canisters
- field backpack
- scanner
- ecology tools

Those are optional equipment layers.

## Approval check
At distance, Noma should be identifiable immediately from the **green palette + softer silhouette + leaf antenna profile**, even completely equipment-free.
