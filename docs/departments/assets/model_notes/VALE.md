# Vale — Current 3D Model Notes

Use the **current Cato 3D model** for shared mechanical/material language, not the old Vale PNG body.

## Identity target
- tall, balanced, approachable support/generalist chassis
- cream/off-white shell
- warm red identity accents; muted blue/blue-gray may remain as a secondary collar/support color if it fits the current family language
- glossy dark visor with highly readable emissive eye shapes
- open, socially readable head/shoulder silhouette
- less industrially massive than Cato despite being nearly as tall

## Proportion target
- approximately 2.00 m presentation cue
- tall but not bulky
- balanced shoulders and limbs
- approachable rather than imposing
- visually closer to a versatile all-rounder than a specialist machine

## Personality read
Warm, attentive, cooperative, adaptable. Vale should have the most naturally social/readable visor presence of the group.

## Important correction
Do **not** lock Vale into a medic identity.

Avoid permanent:
- medical cross
- doctor markings
- dedicated medical backpack
- fixed medical equipment

Support/medical tools can exist later only as real optional equipment.

## Do not bake in
- tablet
- utility bag
- headset
- aid kit
- specialist support gear

Those are optional equipment layers.

## Approval check
At distance, Vale should read as **tall, warm, approachable, red-accented support/generalist**, not as Cato-with-red-paint and not as a permanent medic.
