# Bex — Current 3D Model Notes

Use the **current Cato 3D model** for shared mechanical/material language, not the old Bex sprite geometry.

## Identity target
- compact, hands-on maker/fabricator chassis
- cream/off-white shell with strong orange identity accents
- dark graphite internals
- glossy dark visor with cyan/aqua display language
- short, practical antenna shapes
- slightly more expressive/animated face language than Cato
- sturdy forearms/hands may communicate maker energy through body design alone

## Proportion target
- compact and noticeably shorter than Cato
- approximately 1.60 m presentation cue
- practical, dense silhouette rather than elegant/slender
- **not** a miniature Cato: less shoulder mass, less hauling-body bulk, more nimble workshop feel

## Personality read
Quick-thinking, tactile, inventive, enthusiastic when something can be improved.

## Do not bake in
- tool arm
- fabrication backpack
- belt tools
- drill/saw attachments
- crates/material packs

Those are optional equipment layers.

## Approval check
At distance, Bex should read as **small, orange, practical, maker-like**, with a silhouette clearly different from both Cato's heavy hauling body and Noma's softer field-science body.
