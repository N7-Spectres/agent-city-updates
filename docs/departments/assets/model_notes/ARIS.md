# Aris — Current 3D Model Notes

Use the **current Cato 3D model** for shared mechanical/material language, not the old Aris PNG body.

## Identity target
- lean, agile, field-oriented chassis
- cream/off-white shell with teal/cyan identity accents
- small warm/orange navigation-style accent details are welcome
- glossy dark visor with cyan emissive eyes
- explorer-like head silhouette
- antenna/fins should feel light and directional rather than bulky
- neck/collar/scarf-like identity can remain as a permanent silhouette cue if translated cleanly into the current 3D style

## Proportion target
- visibly narrower and lighter than Cato
- long, agile limb read
- smaller shoulder mass
- approximately 1.85 m presentation cue
- should feel built for movement and fieldwork without implying speed/capability stats

## Personality read
Curious, outward-looking, observant, ready to go see what is over the next ridge.

## Do not bake in
- backpack
- survey tablet
- scanner
- beacon
- prospecting equipment
- navigation gear

Those are optional equipment layers.

## Approval check
At distance, Aris should be identifiable by the **lean explorer silhouette + teal identity + head fins/collar**, even with all gear removed.
